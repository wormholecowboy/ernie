ernie · Style guide

Colours for every ernie page, taken from the Planning tab. One dark look. Values below are read live from _tokens.css; ratios are WCAG contrast against the surface each colour sits on.

Rules

  • SurfacesThree only. The page is brown. Things you work in (epic and bead lists, issue list, question cards, tables) are burnt-orange boxes with a mustard edge. A selected row inside a box is cut back to brown with a teal bar on its left.
  • On orangeOnly cream reads on orange (4.7:1). Anything filled orange (boxes, chips, buttons) turns every text role cream; status shows by glyph shape (○ ◐ ● ⊘). Mustard is allowed for warning glyphs (3:1, fine for icons, not for small text).
  • Colour = meaningTeal = active, selected, recommended, primary action. Mustard = waiting, blocked, impact. Sage = done, verified. Salmon = error, destructive. Colour shows on brown (page or cut-out), never as small text on orange.
  • EdgesMustard edge = a box at rest. Teal edge = the selected box or the focused control. Hairlines between rows use the plain border, never a colour.
  • No textureNo background grid, no gradients. Separation comes from the brown/orange switch and whitespace, not inner borders.

Palette
